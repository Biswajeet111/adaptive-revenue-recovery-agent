\# Customer Recovery Communication Policy



Version: 1.0

Status: Active



\## Purpose



This policy defines communication principles for payment recovery.



\## Communication Objective



Recovery communication should help the customer complete payment without

creating unnecessary friction.



Messages should clearly explain that the previous payment attempt was

unsuccessful and provide an appropriate recovery option.



\## Alternative Payment Method



When an alternative payment method is selected, the customer may be

provided with a secure payment link.



The message should not expose internal failure codes or internal risk

scores.



\## Transparency



The system should not claim that revenue has been recovered merely because

a payment link was created.



Recovery should only be communicated as successful after payment-provider

confirmation.



\## Failed Recovery



If a recovery attempt fails, communication should avoid misleading

statements and should provide the next appropriate action when available.



\## Escalation



Cases requiring manual review should not be represented to the customer

as automatically resolved.



\## Auditability



Communication events should be associated with the relevant transaction

and recovery case.
